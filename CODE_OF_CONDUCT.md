# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this space to be fun, welcoming, and creative. Everyone who participates is expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and kind
- Focusing on what's best for the community
- Giving credit where it's due
- Accepting constructive disagreement gracefully

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Posting inappropriate content
- Spamming or self-promotion without context
- Anything that kills the vibe

## Enforcement

If you see something that breaks the vibe, reach out. We'll handle it case by case.
